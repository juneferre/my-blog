### **Where Your Travel Budget Goes Far (and Where It Doesn't)**

The idea of traveling is always fun to entertain until you see hotel or flight prices. As you know, traveling is expensive and sometimes requires financial planning. Seeing the world and experiencing different cultures has always been something I've dreamt about but as a college student, it can be hard to pull off. While I've read articles about cheap places to go, they never really provide the break down of costs or specific costs, until I found  <a href="https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/" target="_blank">BudgetYourTrip.com</a>. 

<a href="https://www.budgetyourtrip.com/" target="_blank">BudgetYourTrip.com</a> provided great insight on costs for nearly every city or country possible. I had found a Wikipedia Table that listed the top 100 most visted cities in the world in 2016-2018, so I wanted to see which of those most visted places were the cheapest. If you've been following along with my blog posts, my previous blog post was about travel costs and how I scraped the data for those cities. 

I was definitely surprised by some of the findings! If you missed the last post, here are some of the interesting things I found. 

#### Most Expensive City: Las Vegas, USA
#### Cheapest City: Hurghada, Egypt 

#### Top 5 Most Expensive Cities (Avg Daily Cost USD): 
1. Las Vegas, Nevada ($369/day)
2. Orlando, Florida ($368/day)
3. New York City, New York ($363/day)
4. Honolulu, Hawaii($322/day)
5. Los Angeles, California ($300/day)

#### Top 5 Cheapest Cities (Avg Daily Cost USD): 
1. Hurghada, Egypt ($19/day)
2. Jaipur, India ($22/day)
3. Chennai, India ($33/day)
4. Agra, India ($45/day)
5. Kolkata, India ($46/day)

#### Top 5 Most Expensive Countries (Avg Daily Cost USD): 
1. United States of America ($318.71/day)
2. United Kingdom ($274/day)
3. Denmark ($248/day)
4. United Arab Emirates ($243/day)
5. Australia ($241/day)

#### Top 5 Cheapest Countries (Avg Daily Cost USD):
1. Egypt ($19/day)
2. India ($47/day)
3. Saudi Arabia ($53/day)
4. Malaysia ($64.67/day)
5. Vietnam ($68.25/day)
